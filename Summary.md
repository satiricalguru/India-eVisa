India eVisa 2.0 reimagines the critical digital public service gateway for over five million annual international travelers visiting India. Built for the prestigious national initiative Build What Moves India, the platform transforms the friction-heavy legacy visa portal into a world-class, responsive mobile-first experience inspired by modern global standards like the UK ETA and New Zealand NZeTA.

The legacy application process suffered from subcategory confusion, silent photo rejections after seventy-two hours, exposed payment gateway plumbing, and completely opaque application tracking. India eVisa 2.0 solves these fundamental bottlenecks through five citizen-centric architectural innovations:

First, an upfront four-question eligibility engine matches travelers to their optimal visa tier in thirty seconds, detailing tailored document requirements and guaranteed upfront pricing before account creation. Second, real-time AI pre-flight document inspection powered by GPT-5.6 Terra analyzes passport bio-data and portrait quality to preemptively catch framing, glare, and validity flaws that drive visa rejections. Third, a progressive one-question-per-screen wizard built with GPT-5.6 Luna ensures mobile ergonomics with continuous session autosave. Fourth, unified single-number checkout eliminates unexpected banking surcharges. Fifth, a live milestone status tracker features an interactive Judge Time-Travel mode for instant simulated approvals alongside client-side generation of official vector Electronic Travel Authorization certificates with verifiable security watermarks.

OpenAI Codex accelerated full-stack development by generating TypeScript schemas, comprehensive airport checkpost registries, and document validation routines. By combining intuitive government-grade design systems with cutting-edge multimodal artificial intelligence, India eVisa 2.0 delivers an inclusive, dependable, and highly dignified digital front door for all international global visitors traveling to incredible India.
